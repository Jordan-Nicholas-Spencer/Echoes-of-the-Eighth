## Game Description

You wake up in the center of an octagonal structure called The Glade, surrounded by towering walls with no memory of how you arrived. Each day, a new door opens, revealing access to a mysterious maze that must be explored before nightfall.

Your mission is to navigate each maze, locate hidden puzzles, and solve them to unlock access to the next area. But time is your enemy—when the sun sets, dangerous monsters are released into the maze, hunting anyone who remains inside.

### Core Gameplay

Players face a critical choice each day:

* **Speed Run:** Race against the sunset to find and solve the puzzle, then escape before the monsters are released.
* **Night Survival:** Stay in the maze after dark and fight the monsters that emerge, testing your combat skills and courage.

The monsters will only attack if they spot you, adding a stealth element to nighttime exploration.
